# FoodForFun Atlas - Phase H Public About

**Status:** Implemented

**Last Updated:** August 2026

## Scope

Phase H adds the server-rendered `/about` route required by the MVP. It also
adds About to the primary navigation and links the homepage introduction to the
full editorial explanation. The page is static and makes no database request.

## Public editorial information

The page explains what FoodForFun Atlas is and is not, why food is the starting
point, how source material becomes a reviewed Story, and the principles that
guide publication. It describes public-safe Source metadata, private editorial
records, location precision, technology and AI assistance, and continued human
responsibility for published work.

## Corrections and privacy

Visitors can open a correction Issue in the public project repository for
factual errors, incorrect names, location or rights concerns, outdated business
information, and privacy issues. The page explicitly tells visitors not to post
private addresses, personal contact details, or sensitive evidence publicly. A
sensitive report should identify only the affected Atlas page and request a
private follow-up.

## Boundaries

This phase adds no database reads or writes, migration, seed, analytics,
third-party contact service, sitemap, or robots configuration. Those SEO and
communications capabilities remain separate focused tasks.
